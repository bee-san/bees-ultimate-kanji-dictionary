The SVG files bundled in each release ZIP under kanjivg/ are adaptations of the
original KanjiVG data: the stroke <path> geometry is extracted and re-styled
with a lightweight, dependency-free CSS stroke-order animation (reduced-motion
guarded, static fully-drawn glyph as the fallback). All KanjiVG-specific markup
(kvg: namespaced attributes, stroke-number labels, DOCTYPE, comments) is removed
during sanitization.

In accordance with the Share-Alike term, these adaptations are redistributed
under the same Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike licence. The phonetic
families surfaced in entries are read directly from KanjiVG's own kvg:phon
markers; no phonetic relationship is inferred or invented.
